About Us

Welcome to CHERM Resort , a premier hospitality destination located in Nkoowe, 24.1km along the Kampala–Hoima Highway. We proudly serve both local and travelling guests by providing quality accommodation, exceptional dining experiences and outstanding customer service in a peaceful and welcoming environment.

At CHERM Resort, we understand that every guest expects comfort, delicious cuisine and memorable hospitality. Our restaurant offers a wide variety of local and continental meals prepared to the highest standards, while our accommodation, bar, gardens, sauna and steam bath facilities provide the perfect setting for relaxation, business and special occasions.

Whether you are visiting for leisure, business meetings, family gatherings or celebrations, our dedicated team is committed to delivering excellent service, quality hospitality and an unforgettable experience for every guest.
